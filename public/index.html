    Why use Vidget

    Your media. Your machine.
    No strings attached.

    Most "free" online downloaders and converters pay for themselves with ads, trackers, upsells and your data. Vidget runs on hardware you control, so none of that has to happen.

    Start downloading How it works

    Privacy first

    Your files are processed on your own server and never uploaded to a third-party service. There's no analytics, no cookies and no fingerprinting. Thumbnails are even loaded through your server, so your browser never talks to the video site.

    Zero ads

    No banners, pop-unders, fake "Download" buttons or redirect chains. The only download button is the one that actually downloads your file.

    No subscriptions

    No accounts, no "Pro" tier, no daily caps, no watermarks and no "upgrade to get 1080p". Every feature is available to everyone, free.

    Keep real files

    Get an actual file you own, not a stream that can vanish. Save to your device, and keep a copy on the server in Saves/ if you want.

    Fast & full quality

    Powered by yt-dlp and FFmpeg, the same open-source tools the professionals use. You get up to 4K video, lossless audio, and no queue behind other people.

    Open & inspectable

    Every line of code is on GitHub. You don't have to trust a privacy policy when you can read exactly what the software does.

    About

    About Vidget

    Vidget is a small, self-hosted web app for downloading online video and converting media files. It was built as a private, ad-free and subscription-free alternative to online converter sites.

    How it works

    1. You paste a link or drop a file

      Your browser only talks to this Vidget server.

    2. Your server does the work

      yt-dlp fetches the media straight from the source site, and FFmpeg handles merging and converting.

    3. You get the file

      It downloads straight to your device. Temporary files are cleaned up automatically.

    Where your files go

    • Downloads are saved on the server in Saves/video and Saves/audio, and also sent to your browser.
    • Video conversions: the uploaded file is deleted as soon as conversion finishes, and the result is removed about 30 minutes later.
    • Image uploads are kept in Saves/Uploaded. GIF frame previews are removed after 30 minutes.
    • Settings & history live only in this browser's local storage.
