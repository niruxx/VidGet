          Why use Vidget

          Free, private downloads.
          No strings attached.

          Most "free" online downloaders and converters pay for themselves with ads, trackers, upsells and your data. Vidget is open-source software that doesn't need to make money from you, so none of that has to happen.

          Start downloading How it works

          Privacy first

          Your files are processed on this server, never handed to a third-party service, and deleted shortly after you download them. There's no analytics, no cookies and no fingerprinting. Thumbnails are even loaded through this server, so your browser never talks to the video site.

          Zero ads

          No banners, pop-unders, fake "Download" buttons or redirect chains. The only download button is the one that actually downloads your file.

          No subscriptions

          No accounts, no "Pro" tier, no daily caps, no watermarks and no "upgrade to get 1080p". Every feature is available to everyone, free.

          Keep real files

          Get an actual file you own, not a stream that can vanish. Save it to your device and keep it for as long as you like.

          Fast & full quality

          Powered by yt-dlp and FFmpeg, the same open-source tools the professionals use. You get up to 4K video and lossless audio, with no paid "fast lane".

          Open & inspectable

          Every line of code is on GitHub. You don't have to trust a privacy policy when you can read exactly what the software does.

          About

          About Vidget

          Vidget is a small, self-hosted web app for downloading online video and converting media files. It was built as a private, ad-free and subscription-free alternative to online converter sites. No account needed.

          How it works

          1. You paste a link or drop a file

            Your browser only talks to this Vidget server.

          2. This server does the work

            yt-dlp fetches the media straight from the source site, and FFmpeg handles merging and converting.

          3. You get the file

            It downloads straight to your device. Temporary files are cleaned up automatically.

          Privacy & terms

          What this server does with your data

          This page is generated from this server's live settings, so it always matches what actually happens.

          What is stored

            What is never collected

            • No accounts, sign-ups or email addresses.
            • No cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or ads.
            • No third-party scripts, fonts or CDNs. Everything on this page comes from this server.
            • Your settings and history stay in your browser's local storage and are never uploaded.

            Who else is contacted

            • The site you link to. This server fetches the media from it, so that site sees this server's address, not yours.
            • Thumbnails are also loaded through this server, so your browser never contacts the source site.
            • SponsorBlock (sponsor.ajay.app) is asked for a video's sponsor segments only when you tick “Cut out sponsor segments”. Only the video's ID is sent.

            Limits on this server

            Acceptable use & copyright

            Only download or convert content you have the right to: your own uploads, content with a permissive license, or content where the law in your country allows a personal copy. Don't use this server to infringe copyright, bypass DRM or paywalls, or harass anyone.
